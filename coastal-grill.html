Our Story

Harbor-side, since day one.

The Coastal Grill started as a small grill stand on the San Diego waterfront, serving the fishing crews coming in at dawn. Three generations later, the open flame is still the heart of the kitchen.

We work with local fishmongers and a small produce farm inland, keeping the menu short so everything stays fresh.
